## 1. Data We Collect

**From GitHub OAuth (on login):**
GitHub username, GitHub user ID, public profile avatar, and (if you grant repository access
during the seller "Connect Repos" flow) an encrypted GitHub access token and the list of
repositories you choose to connect.

**Provided directly by you (during onboarding):**
- Full legal name
- Phone number
- Profile picture (if different from your GitHub avatar)

**Generated by your use of the Platform:**
- Listings you create, including descriptions, pricing, and tech stack details
- Purchase and order history, both as a buyer and as a seller
- Balance, payout requests, and payout destination (e.g. last 4 digits of a card)
- Comments, ratings, and reviews you post
- Reports you submit, and reports submitted about you (visible to our moderation team, not to
  other users)
- Device/log data: IP address, browser type, timestamps, and similar technical metadata collected
  automatically for security and anti-abuse purposes (including via Cloudflare's security services)

**From third parties:**
- Payment confirmation data from our payment processor ([PROVIDER NAME]) — we receive confirmation
  that a payment succeeded/failed, not your full card details, which are handled directly by the
  processor.

## 2. Why We Collect It

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| GitHub identity, avatar | Account creation, login, displaying your seller/buyer profile |
| Legal name, phone number | Identity verification, fraud prevention, communicating about orders/payouts, complying with applicable financial regulations |
| Listings, orders, balance | Operating the marketplace itself — matching buyers and sellers, processing payments, calculating payouts |
| Reports, moderation data | Investigating misconduct, enforcing our Terms of Use, protecting other users from fraud or malicious code |
| Device/log data | Security, fraud detection, rate limiting, and preventing abuse of the Platform |

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

## 3. Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable law requires a stated legal basis, we rely on: your consent (given when you accept
these terms during onboarding), the necessity of processing to perform our contract with you (i.e.
operating your marketplace account), and our legitimate interest in preventing fraud and abuse on
the Platform.

## 4. Who We Share Data With

- **GitHub** — for authentication; governed by GitHub's own privacy policy for data you share with them.
- **[Payment Processor Name]** — to process purchases and payouts.
- **Cloudflare** — for security, DDoS protection, and bot/CAPTCHA verification (Turnstile); this
  involves processing of your IP address and device signals at the network edge.
- **Law enforcement or regulators** — where required by a valid legal request under the laws of the
  Republic of Uzbekistan or another applicable jurisdiction.
- **Other users, in limited form** — your GitHub username, avatar, seller bio, and listing history

  **not** shown to other users and are used internally only.
